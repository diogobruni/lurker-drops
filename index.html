What it does

Small helper. Big relief.

It is like asking a friend to keep the TV on the right channel. Except this friend never gets bored.

  • Pick the show

    Choose a category, like a game or Just Chatting. Add a word the title must have, or words you never want to see.

  • One preset per tab

    Cozy streams in one tab, your favorite game in another. Each tab remembers its own rules, favorites, and blacklist.

  • Finds a live for you

    Every minute it looks again. If a bigger stream fits your rules, the tab moves there. Turn Pursue off to stay on your favorites.

  • Quiet watching

    Mute the tab while it watches. Your speakers stay free for calls, music, or the video you are actually watching.

  • Chat rescue

    If Twitch chat disconnects, the extension reconnects it. If that fails, it reloads the page, at most once every five minutes.

  • Bonus chests

    When a bonus chest appears on the page, it clicks it for you. No more remembering to look.

How it works

Three steps. Then forget about it.

You set the rules once. The extension does the boring part, all day long.

  1. 01

    Tell it what to watch

    Open the popup, pick a category, and add a filter if you want. Save it as a preset to reuse later.

  2. 02

    Press On

    The tab opens a live that matches your rules and stays there. The icon shows how many lurkers are active.

  3. 03

    Go live your life

    Work, study, or play. It checks again every minute and keeps the right stream playing until you turn it off.

You can run several tabs at the same time, each with a different preset.

Install

Add it to your browser in about a minute

Lurker Drops is now in the Chrome Web Store. Install it with one click, or use the GitHub release if you prefer a manual install.

Add to Chrome

Works on desktop Chrome, Edge, Opera, and Brave. The store link opens the official Chrome Web Store page.

  1. 1

    Open the store page

    The button above opens the official Chrome Web Store page for Lurker Drops.

  2. 2

    Click Add to Chrome

    Confirm the permissions. The extension needs access to Twitch pages to keep the tab watching and to reconnect chat.

  3. 3

    Pin and press On

    Pin Lurker Drops to the toolbar, open the popup, add your first lurker, and press On.

Manual install from GitHub

Prefer a manual install? Use the official release ZIP and Developer mode. Keep the ZIP file as it is.

Download the latest release
  1. 1

    Download the ZIP

    The download always serves the newest release from GitHub.

  2. 2

    Open the extensions page and turn on Developer mode

    Use chrome://extensions on Chrome and Brave, edge://extensions on Edge, or opera://extensions on Opera. The switch is in the top-right corner.

  3. 3

    Drag the ZIP onto the page

    Drop the ZIP anywhere on the extensions page and confirm. If that fails, unzip it and use Load unpacked with the folder.

  4. 4

    Pin and press On

    Pin Lurker Drops, open the popup, and press On.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Lurker Drops is open source. There is no account, no subscription, no ads, and nothing to unlock.

Do I need to keep the browser open?

Yes. The tab must stay open for Twitch to count the watch time. You can mute it, send it to the background, or move it to another window.

Will it keep changing channels?

Only the tab it manages. With Pursue on, it moves to a matching live with more viewers. Turn Pursue off to follow your favorites in the order you set.

Does it collect my data?

No analytics, no tracking, and no server. Your settings stay in browser storage on your computer. It only asks Twitch for the public list of live streams.

Does it guarantee drops or rewards?

No. It keeps the tab watching a live that matches your rules, but drops and rewards follow Twitch and campaign rules. Never use it to break those rules.

Which browsers work?

Chrome, Edge, Opera, Brave, and other Chromium-based browsers on desktop. Firefox is not supported.
